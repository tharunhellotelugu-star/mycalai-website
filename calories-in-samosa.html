Calories in a samosa

One standard deep-fried potato samosa (about 50g) runs roughly 130–160 calories. It looks like a small snack, but the pastry shell absorbs a surprising amount of oil during frying — often more than the potato filling itself contributes.

Where the calories come from

Fried pastry shell~60% of calories75–95 kcal
Potato-pea filling (spiced)~40% of calories55–65 kcal
Total (1 samosa, ~50g)~130–160 kcal
2 samosas~260–320 kcal

The filling itself — boiled potato, peas, and spices — is fairly light on its own. Nearly all of a samosa's calorie density comes from deep-frying the maida (refined flour) pastry, which absorbs oil throughout the shell rather than just on the surface. This is why a samosa's calorie count doesn't scale simply with how "big" the filling looks.

Samosas are carb-and-fat forward with very little protein, which is normal for a fried pastry snack — the nutritional profile is closer to a fried appetizer than a meal component, even though it's often eaten alongside a full meal.

Baked vs. fried, and what chutney adds

  • Baked samosa: ~70–100 kcal each — saves 40–60 calories by avoiding oil absorption in the shell, though the texture is noticeably different.
  • Air-fried samosa: ~90–120 kcal each — a middle ground, using a fraction of the oil of deep frying.
  • Tamarind or mint chutney: Adds 15–30 calories per 1–2 tablespoons — small individually, but easy to have several spoonfuls without noticing.

Why "how many samosas" is the real question here

Unlike a curry or biryani, a samosa's calorie count per piece is fairly consistent — the harder part for calorie tracking is usually just the count. One samosa eaten as a snack is a very different total than three eaten as a light meal, and it's easy to lose track of the number when they're small and eaten quickly, especially at a gathering or from a shared plate.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in 2 samosas?

Two standard fried samosas come to roughly 260–320 calories, before counting any chutney served alongside.

Is a baked samosa lower in calories than a fried one?

Yes, meaningfully — a baked samosa typically saves 40–60 calories compared to a deep-fried one of the same size, since it isn't absorbing oil through the pastry during cooking.

Which has more calories, a samosa or a spring roll?

They're usually fairly close — both are fried pastry with a filling — though a samosa's thicker dough often makes it slightly higher in calories than a thin-wrapper spring roll of similar size.
