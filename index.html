Questions

Frequently asked questions.

How does MyCal AI count calories from a photo?

MyCal AI uses an AI vision model to identify every food item visible in a single plate photo at once, not just one item at a time. It estimates each item's portion size and calculates calories, protein, carbs, and fat for the whole plate, then logs it to your food diary automatically.

What makes MyCal AI different from other AI calorie tracker apps?

Most AI food scanner apps identify a single dominant item per photo. MyCal AI performs multi-item detection, separately identifying and estimating nutrition for every distinct food on your plate in one scan, whether it's a mixed thali, a multi-dish meal, or a plate with several sides.

Does MyCal AI work well for Indian and regional cuisines?

Yes. MyCal AI's model is built with deep knowledge across global cuisines, including Indian regional dishes, and is tuned to distinguish visually similar dishes rather than defaulting to generic Western food guesses.

Is there a free version of MyCal AI?

Yes. MyCal AI offers a free tier with a limited number of AI photo scans per day, plus the option to earn bonus scans by watching a short ad. Premium unlocks significantly more daily scans and unlimited access to the AI Nutrition Coach.

Can MyCal AI track macros, not just calories?

Yes. Every scanned meal breaks down calories, protein, carbs, fat, fiber, and sodium per item, and MyCal AI tracks daily and weekly macro trends, not just a single calorie total.

Does MyCal AI have an AI coach, not just a food log?

Yes. The built-in AI Nutrition Coach answers questions using your actual logged meal history, so advice is based on what you've really eaten, not a generic script.
