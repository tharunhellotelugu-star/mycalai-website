Why generic calorie counters struggle with Indian meals

Barcode-first apps assume packaged, single-item food. A home-cooked Indian plate rarely works that way — it's several dishes sharing a plate, with regional variation in oil, portion, and preparation that a generic food database can't capture. MyCal AI's AI vision model was trained with deep knowledge of Indian regional cuisine, so it recognizes dishes by appearance and separates them individually rather than lumping the whole plate into one rough guess.

Frequently asked questions

Can an AI calorie counter actually recognize Indian dishes accurately?

MyCal AI's model is trained with deep knowledge of Indian regional cuisine, so it distinguishes similar-looking dishes (e.g. dal vs sambar, different rice preparations) rather than defaulting to a generic Western guess.

How does MyCal AI handle a mixed thali with multiple items?

MyCal AI performs multi-item detection, identifying each dish on a thali separately and estimating calories and macros per item, then totaling the plate — instead of guessing one dominant food.
