How MyCal AI's scanner works

  • Multi-item segmentation — every distinct food on the plate is identified separately, with its own calorie and macro estimate.
  • Portion-aware estimates — nutrition is estimated based on visible portion size per item, not a flat per-dish average.
  • Automatic logging — once confirmed, the scan logs straight to your daily food diary with a full macro breakdown.
  • Barcode scanning — for packaged products, scan the barcode for an instant, exact lookup instead of an AI estimate.

Built for real meals, not lab conditions

Food photo AI models are usually benchmarked on single, clearly separated foods. MyCal AI is tuned for the messier, more common case — a plate with several foods touching or overlapping — because that's what most people actually eat and photograph.

Frequently asked questions

What is an AI food scanner app?

An AI food scanner app uses a photo of your meal to automatically identify the food and estimate its nutrition, instead of manually searching a food database or scanning a barcode.

Does MyCal AI scan more than one food item at a time?

Yes. MyCal AI performs multi-item detection, identifying every distinct food on the plate in a single photo rather than picking just the most prominent item.

Does MyCal AI still support barcode scanning?

Yes, for packaged products a barcode scanner is available for an instant lookup alongside the AI plate scan.
