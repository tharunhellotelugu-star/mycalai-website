Nutrition Guide

How many calories in 2 rotis?

Two plain whole wheat rotis (no ghee or oil, about 40g each) come to roughly 170–200 calories. Add a light brushing of ghee to each, which is extremely common, and that climbs to 220–260 calories — a bigger jump than most people expect from something that looks like a small amount.

Plain vs. buttered roti

TypeCalories (2 rotis, ~80g total)
Plain (dry, no oil/ghee)170–200 kcal
Lightly brushed with ghee220–260 kcal
Generously buttered (restaurant-style)260–310 kcal

The flour, water, and salt base of a roti is very consistent — it's genuinely one of the easier foods to estimate accurately, plain or dry-roasted. The variable is entirely the ghee or oil brushed on afterward, and because it's often a thin, even coating rather than a visible pool, it's easy to miss both by eye and in a photo.

Roti vs. naan vs. paratha vs. rice

These four get compared constantly, and the differences are bigger than people assume:

  • Roti (plain): ~85–100 kcal each — the lightest of the four.
  • Roti with ghee: ~110–130 kcal each.
  • Naan: ~250–300 kcal each — leavened with yeast/yogurt and usually brushed with butter, plus it's typically larger than a roti.
  • Paratha (plain, layered, pan-fried): ~200–260 kcal each — the layering technique itself requires oil or ghee between folds, not just on the surface.
  • Paratha (stuffed, e.g. aloo paratha): ~250–350 kcal each, depending on filling and oil.
  • Steamed rice: ~130 kcal per half-cup (65g) — comparable to 1–1.5 rotis depending on preparation.

If you're trying to reduce calories without giving up an Indian-meal staple, plain roti is consistently the lightest option among these, by a meaningful margin over naan or paratha.

Why "how many rotis" isn't the full question

Calorie tracking for roti-based meals often goes wrong not because of the roti count, but because of what's on the roti — the ghee brushed on afterward, or what it's used to scoop up (a rich gravy versus a dry sabzi). Two rotis eaten dry with a simple dal is a very different calorie total than two rotis used to mop up a buttery paneer gravy, even though "two rotis" sounds identical in both descriptions.

This is why MyCal AI asks (or infers from a photo) about preparation specifics like this rather than treating "2 rotis" as a single fixed number — a dry-roasted roti and a ghee-brushed one are genuinely different foods calorically, even though they look almost the same.

Frequently asked questions

Is roti good for weight loss?

Yes, generally — plain roti is moderate in calories, high in fiber relative to naan or paratha, and a reasonable portion (2–3 rotis) fits comfortably into most calorie targets, especially without added ghee.

How many calories in 4 rotis?

Roughly double the 2-roti figures: about 340–400 calories plain, or 440–520 with ghee.

Which is better for weight loss, roti or rice?

Both can fit a weight-loss plan — roti generally has a bit more fiber and protein per calorie, while rice is easier to portion precisely. The bigger factor for either is usually what you eat them with.
