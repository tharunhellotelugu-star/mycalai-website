Frequently asked questions

Why don't barcode-based calorie trackers work for home-cooked food?

Barcode scanners only work for packaged products with a printed barcode. Home-cooked meals, restaurant plates, and mixed dishes have no barcode, so those apps require manual search and guesswork for most real meals.

How does MyCal AI log a meal without a barcode?

You take one photo of your plate. The AI identifies every food item, estimates portions, and calculates calories and macros automatically, then logs it to your diary.

Can I still use a barcode for packaged snacks?

Yes, MyCal AI includes a barcode scanner for packaged products alongside the AI photo scan, so you can use whichever fits the food in front of you.
